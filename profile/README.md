## MachGTM

Open-source sales tools that show their sources.

We build [MachGTM](https://machgtm.com), an AI sales teammate for B2B teams that sell software and software services. It learns what you sell, researches your leads, writes what to say, reads every reply and books the meeting. You approve what goes out.

### Open source

- **[Lead Finder](https://github.com/MachGTM/lead-finder)**: a free plugin for Claude that finds B2B companies that fit your customer and are buying now, with a source for every claim. MIT.
- **[Receipts](https://machgtm.github.io/receipts/)**: an open standard, a free checker and open data for sales messages written with AI. No source, no send. [Try the check](https://machgtm.github.io/receipts/check/).
